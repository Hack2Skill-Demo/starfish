Gate 2

Certainty: all five must hold

  1. Cause from structure, not guesswork
  2. Fails before the fix, passes after
  3. Verify gate green
  4. Adversarial review finds nothing blocking
  5. Counter-check confirms it's real

Fast-revert safety net

minutes of the fix going live
