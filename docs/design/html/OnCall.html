When Starfish calls

All of these must be true:

If nobody answers

  1. 1Call the primary. Retry once after 2 minutes
  2. 2Then the secondary
  3. 3Then the support lead
  4. ✉Voicemail: a short message, no incident details; the details are in Slack

Every attempt and its outcome shows up in the run's timeline.

The recorded message

on-call-alert.mp3 · 0:21

“Hello, this is Starfish, the automated ops assistant for [your team]. An urgent incident needs a person, and nobody has picked it up in Slack. The details are in the on-call channel. Press 1 to take it. Press 2 to get the link by text. Press 3 to pass it to the next person.”

Keypad

1Take it: marks the incident acknowledged by you
2Text me the link to the Slack thread
3Pass it to the next person on the list

No key pressed, or hung up: counts as not answered.

Why recorded: the call never says anything about the incident, so no customer data, secrets or log text can leak by voice, and nothing in an error message can steer it. It also can't approve, merge or change anything. The details stay in Slack, where the team already works.
